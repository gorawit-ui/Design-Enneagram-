# Core 2 Emotional Moments — Production Handoff

## Scope and product contract

This documentation-only handoff defines the proposed Core 2 pilot: four
emotional moments in Female, Male, and Neutral presentations, for 12 future
assets total. It does not authorize image creation or integration.

- Enneagram Core defines the emotional baseline.
- MBTI deterministically selects one illustration moment; the existing Scene
  Kit supplies the work context.
- Wing may add only a small accent inside an existing scene object.
- A/T may affect ambient tone only (`settled` or `reflective`). It must never
  change facial emotion, distress, or mental-health meaning.
- Presentation changes only approved face and hair details. Female, Male, and
  Neutral retain identical body proportions, pose, hands, outfit, prop,
  lighting, crop, and expression intensity.
- Personality must never be encoded through body shape, weight, build,
  ability, gender, competence, rank, or attractiveness.

## Core 2 art direction

All four moments retain Core 2's warm, attentive emotional baseline and the
same full-body character system. Differences must be readable at review size,
natural, and restrained rather than theatrical.

| Moment | Required reading | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| `observant-curiosity` | Focused eyes, relaxed brow, and a thoughtful, attentive presence. | Coldness, suspicion, surveillance, detachment, or intellectual superiority. |
| `warm-engagement` | Soft eyes and a small natural smile that reads as present and open. | Servility, flirtation, ingratiation, rescuing, or exaggerated sweetness. |
| `idea-spark` | Alert eyes and contained delight, with energy held inside the shared pose. | Hyperactivity, childishness, manic coding, novelty-as-superiority, or a broad performative grin. |
| `grounded-action` | Direct attention, steady brow, and composed mouth; ready without strain. | Aggression, dominance, toughness, anger, command posture, or physical-power coding. |

Across moments, keep body proportions, pose, hands, outfit, prop, lighting,
crop, and overall expression intensity locked. Moment distinction comes from
the approved facial treatment, not a new body story or work-role stereotype.

## Prohibited treatments

- No personality, emotion, capability, seniority, health, employability, or
  desirability cue through body shape, weight, build, ability, gender,
  complexion, apparent status, or attractiveness.
- No presentation-specific change to body proportions, pose, hands, outfit,
  prop, lighting, crop, or expression intensity.
- No type may appear happier, smarter, stronger, more competent, more senior,
  more employable, or more emotionally healthy than another.
- No cold or suspicious `observant-curiosity`; no servile or flirtatious
  `warm-engagement`; no hyperactive or childish `idea-spark`; no aggressive or
  dominant `grounded-action`.
- No distress, diagnosis, anxiety, instability, mental-health claim, pity,
  threat, seduction, infantilization, or caricature.
- No MBTI-, Wing-, or A/T-specific face, body, pose, gesture, or expression.
- No Wing accent outside an existing Scene Kit object and no Wing-created role,
  hierarchy, or new focal story.
- No A/T change beyond restrained ambient tone; `settled` and `reflective` must
  be equal in emotional intensity and value.
- No embedded background, Scene Kit, text, logo, type label, or participant
  data in any PNG.
