## Product intent

Characters should feel alive through deterministic differences in gaze, facial expression, body pose, gesture, and observable action that are inspired by MBTI and Enneagram Wing context. The system is a reflection aid, not a diagnosis or an employment decision tool.

## Locked boundaries

- Enneagram Core defines emotional baseline and inner motive.
- MBTI defines observable attention style, pose family, gesture, action, and Scene Kit context.
- Wing changes only a small accent inside an existing object/action; it must not change body shape or status.
- A/T changes ambient tone only (settled/reflective); never distress, pathology, or facial problems.
- Female, Male, and Neutral presentations keep identical pose, hands, outfit, prop placement, lighting, crop, and expression intensity; only approved face/hair presentation may vary.
- All characters use balanced adult proportions. Never encode personality, competence, rank, attractiveness, gender, weight, or ability through body shape.
- Ambiguous or low-confidence results use a neutral pose/expression and must not imply certainty.
- Module 1 scoring, question flow, 20-question limit, and existing result contract remain frozen.

## Recommended model

Define behavior for all 16 MBTI types, but group production art into four controlled pose families to keep the first release maintainable:

1. observant-curiosity: balanced stance, attention toward a map/object, small analytical gesture.
2. warm-engagement: open shoulders, welcoming hand/recipient orientation, natural listening posture.
3. idea-spark: slight exploratory lean, hand linking or presenting a possibility, contained delight.
4. grounded-action: stable stance, direct attention to a plan/object, composed readiness to act.

Each MBTI row must contain: type, behavior rationale, emotional moment, pose family, gaze cue, gesture cue, observable action, primary scene object, prohibited reading, and accessibility description. Do not use model letters or stereotypes in participant-facing copy.

## Pilot scope

Pilot the four existing Gate C types first: INTJ, ISTJ, ENFP, and ESFP with Core 2 and all three presentations. The pilot is 4 types × 3 presentations = 12 candidate assets. Core 2 baseline, Wing accent, and A/T ambient behavior must remain visible without changing the pose contract.

Do not expand to all 16 types or all 9 Cores until the pilot passes the pose, parity, bias, responsive, and technical gates.
